Solution · colloid · suspension

Larger suspension grains sink faster. Solutions and colloids never settle.

Magnify exaggerates particle size so you can compare them. Real sizes differ by thousands of times.

Same solvent. Different particle size.
Appearance Transparent Cloudy Cloudy
Settles? No No Yes

Tyndall effect No Yes Yes

Examples Salt water, sugar water Milk, fog, gelatin Muddy water, sand in water

Dissolved ions stay evenly spread and do not scatter a beam. Colloid particles jitter (Brownian motion) and stay up. Suspension particles sink into sediment and can be caught by filter paper.
